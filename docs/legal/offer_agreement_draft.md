# Offer / User Agreement Draft

Status: draft for closed pilot. Requires legal review before public launch.

Updated: 2026-06-14

## Service

Domens Radar helps users discover domain candidates, maintain personal watchlists, receive explainable alerts, and explicitly confirm registration actions.

## Closed Pilot Mode

The current service state is suitable for a closed pilot with trusted users. Public paid promotion and promises of automatic safe registration are blocked until:

- legal documents are approved;
- production canary is recorded;
- registration safety gate is accepted;
- support contacts are published.

## User Responsibilities

The user should independently verify domain name suitability, trademark risks, spelling, and commercial use constraints before registration.

## Domain Availability

Domain availability can change quickly. Provider responses can be delayed, stale, incomplete, or unavailable. A domain suggestion or alert is not a guarantee that the domain can be registered.

## Registration Actions

Registration must be explicit. The service should create a registration order only after user confirmation. If registration is disabled by configuration, execution is blocked.

DNS zone creation, DNS reserve, or hosting setup is not equal to domain registration and must not be represented as a purchased domain.

## Fees And Payments

Pricing, payment process, refund rules, taxes, and registrar fees are not finalized in this draft.

## Prohibited Use

Do not use the service for:

- abusive bulk registration attempts;
- phishing, impersonation, trademark abuse, or illegal content;
- attempts to overload provider APIs or Telegram delivery;
- actions that violate registrar or platform terms.

## Liability Draft

The service provides suggestions and operational tooling. Final legal, trademark, commercial, and registration decisions remain with the user. The final limitation of liability must be reviewed by counsel.

## Required Before Public Launch

- Add legal entity or operator details.
- Add accepted payment terms if paid flows are enabled.
- Add support and dispute process.
- Add refund/cancellation policy.
- Add final limitation of liability.
- Complete legal review.
