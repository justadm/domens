## Product Disclaimer

Domens Radar is a domain discovery and alerting tool. It does not guarantee that a suggested domain is legally safe, commercially suitable, trademark-free, or available at the moment the user acts.

## Registration Disclaimer

Registration is disabled by default until explicitly enabled by operators. Any registration flow must require fresh availability checks and explicit user confirmation.

Provider responses can be stale or inconclusive. DNS zone reserve or DNS hosting setup is not a completed domain registration.

## Alert Disclaimer

Scores and explanations are ranking signals, not legal or business advice. The user should verify:

- domain availability with the registrar;
- trademark conflicts;
- spelling and brand risks;
- TLD rules and restrictions;
- final price and renewal terms.

## Closed Pilot Notice

During closed pilot, users should expect conservative alert limits, manual review of registration flows, and possible changes to scoring, suppression, and feedback behavior.
